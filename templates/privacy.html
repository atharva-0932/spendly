Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account and use Spendly, we collect the following information:

  • Account information — your name and email address provided during registration.
  • Expense data — transaction amounts, categories, dates, and any descriptions you choose to enter.
  • Usage data — basic information about how you interact with the Service, such as pages visited and actions taken, used solely to improve the product.

We do not collect payment information, government-issued identification, or any data beyond what is necessary to operate the Service.

2. How We Use Your Data

The data we collect is used exclusively to provide and improve the Spendly experience:

  • To authenticate your account and keep it secure.
  • To display your expense history, summaries, and category breakdowns.
  • To improve the reliability, performance, and features of the Service.

We will never sell, rent, or trade your personal or financial data to third parties. We will never use your data to serve you advertisements.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secured servers. We apply industry-standard measures including encryption in transit (HTTPS) and access controls to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us. Upon confirmed deletion, your data will be permanently removed from our systems within 30 days.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may use trusted third-party services to help operate the platform — for example, hosting providers or database infrastructure. These providers are bound by confidentiality agreements and are only permitted to process your data as necessary to support the Service.

We do not integrate with or share data with advertising networks, data brokers, or social media platforms. Any future third-party integrations will be clearly disclosed in an update to this policy.

5. Cookies

Spendly uses session cookies solely to keep you logged in between page visits. These cookies contain no personally identifiable information beyond a secure session token, and are deleted when you log out or close your browser session.

We do not use tracking cookies, analytics cookies, or any cookies from third parties.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please get in touch via the contact information available on our website. We take privacy seriously and will respond promptly.
